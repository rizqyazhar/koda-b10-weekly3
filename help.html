How it works

How does WargaReport get city issues fixed?

  1. You spot an issue (pothole, water leak, dark street) and snap a photo.
  2. WargaReport verifies the evidence and dispatches it directly to the responsible municipal agency (PAM JAYA, Bina Marga, Dishub, DLH, Satpol PP, Citata).
  3. A public countdown starts. Neighbors can upvote the report to increase priority.
  4. Field crews resolve the physical issue and post verification proof before closing.
